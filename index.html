Shikang(Danny) Wu   /   吴世康

Shikang Wu is a Tech Lead for Douyin Search Ranking, leading the direction of the Douyin Next-Generation Search Engine. His work focuses on building a next-generation search engine based on a Generative Search Foundation Model and a Large Recommendation Model.

Prior to joining Douyin, he was a Senior ML Scientist at Baidu Search. He received his M.S. and B.E. degrees from Beijing Jiaotong University, advised by Zhihao Wu and Youfang Lin.

Research Interests

Shikang's research interests include recommender systems and information retrieval. More specifically, his research focuses on:

  • Large-scale recommendation models inspired by recent progress in LLMs and VLMs, emphasizing performance improvements through scaling model capacity.
  • The integration of multimodal signals and personalization in recommender systems.
  • Representation learning, particularly graph representation learning.
  • Generative recommendation models, including sequential and multimodal paradigms.
